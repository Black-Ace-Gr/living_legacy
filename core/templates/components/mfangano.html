Chapter Six

Mfangano

Class Six · Just Mum and Me

For a while, it was just the two of us.

Mum was working in Mfangano Island, and I was doing my schooling there.

I remember going fishing. I remember learning to swim. I remember coming home with fish and watching Mum fry them.

They were ordinary days.

But some ordinary days become extraordinary memories.
MFANGANO ISLAND

Later, Mum decided the education I was receiving wasn't good enough.

So she brought me home. Because that's what she always did. She looked out for us.
